WHAT IS REAL / WHAT IS STILL A PROTOTYPE

Own the workflow.
Not the carrier network.

The routing engine, duplicate protection, trainer digests, stale-draft handling, local inbox, clipboard control, and phone-number SMS link are working code.

SMS delivery is not automated. You copy the draft, open Messages, paste, and tap Send. Your device chooses SMS, RCS, or iMessage according to its settings; this website cannot force the transport or read the result.

This public staging demo has no authenticated owner session and no live client data. A role-view button is not a security boundary. Real owner-only access, verified opted-in trainer contacts, server-side events, database storage, background expiry checks, and remote trainer inboxes must be integrated and tested before production.

No SMS provider, AI API, push provider, phone-number rental, or paid subscription is activated here. This does not waive carrier charges or hosting costs.

State stays in this browser tab's session storage where available. Do not enter private client information. Reset clears the local demonstration.
