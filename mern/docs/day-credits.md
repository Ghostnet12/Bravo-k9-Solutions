# Training membership credits

Staff, administrators and owners use the client's calendar. In **Add or Cancel Date**, select one or more saved training dates, check **Give credit for this day / selected days**, optionally add a note, and save. All selected dates must belong to the same training membership. The save cancels their training visits and gives one credit per distinct missed date in one transaction. Past and already-cancelled training dates remain eligible, but a missed date cannot be credited twice.

Weekend access starts off. New credits are placed after the last covered day, skipping Saturdays and Sundays unless the operator explicitly includes weekends. Only actual credit dates receive the Credit label; skipped weekend dates are not credits. Coverage extends through the full last credit date, including any gap needed to reach a weekday. Existing weekend visits can still be cancelled, and existing weekend credits can still be selected and moved while weekends are off.

Tap a **Credit** day on either the saved calendar or editing calendar, choose **Move credit**, select the new date and save. Notes are optional. Moving keeps the credit count and original audit dates unchanged. It extends coverage when necessary; moving earlier never shortens paid access or cancels a booked visit. Moving a credit does not move a scheduled appointment. A weekend destination requires explicitly checking **Include weekends**. Duplicate destinations, stale revisions and overlaps with a later membership are rejected.

**Add credit days** lets an operator add extra credits without cancelling visits. It supports 1–366 days per save, with no lifetime total limit; further saves can add more. The form defaults to weekdays and an optional note. Both direct additions and cancellation credits use the same placement rules and membership updates.

`POST /api/client-schedule/credits` accepts a client, term, expected end, request UUID, day count, optional missedDates (legacy missedDate remains supported), includeWeekends (default false), and optional note/reason. The count must equal the number of unique missed dates when cancelling. `POST /api/client-schedule/credits/move` accepts clientId, creditId, expectedRevision, from, to, requestKey, includeWeekends and optional note. Both routes require staff permissions, same-origin writes, rate limits and a serialized database transaction. Retries are idempotent; stale and concurrent moves cannot duplicate credits.

Ledger entries now store explicit creditDates. Entries without that field retain their original derived dates until an operator moves them; no existing credits are automatically relocated. Moves preserve the other dates in a multi-day credit entry and append an audit trail. Training subscriptions, linked booking coverage, matching manual member/online access and expiry reminders update together. Clients see the placement dates and receive notifications. No payment, refund, trainer reassignment or automatic appointment is created.

Verification includes unit coverage for weekdays, DST, month boundaries and legacy placements; database tests for batch rollback, retries, permissions, concurrent moves, access extensions, renewal conflicts and unchanged booked visits; and Chrome/WebKit checks for legacy Sunday edits, unchecked weekend controls, bulk cancellation credits, movement from both calendars across months, extra credits and client visibility.
