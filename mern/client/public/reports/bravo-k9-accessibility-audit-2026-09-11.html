Executive summary

Bravo K9 Solutions reviewed its public marketing, booking, account, learning, messaging, and accessibility-statement experiences. The reviewed production release passed 78 automated regression and security tests, compiled successfully, loaded on all eight sampled public routes without horizontal overflow, and produced no recent runtime errors during the review window.

Important: This is an engineering and operational accessibility report, not a legal opinion, government certification, or guarantee that every future page state and third-party service will be barrier-free.

Standard and approach

Bravo uses the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Levels A and AA, as its primary design and testing goal. Practical enhancements include larger important controls, stronger form boundaries, reduced-motion support, and user-selectable contrast and text sizing.

The review used WCAG-EM concepts: define the scope, explore the website, select representative pages and processes, evaluate them, and document the results. Automated checks were paired with code inspection, live keyboard checks, contrast measurement, zoom and responsive-layout testing.

Improvements published

  • Added predictable focus entry, Escape-to-close, focus return, route-change close, and focus-leave behavior to the accessibility panel.
  • Added scroll clearance so sticky headers and controls are less likely to obscure keyboard focus.
  • Raised form-control boundary contrast from 2.73:1 to 4.71:1.
  • Standardized 44-pixel footer link targets.
  • Added recognized input-purpose tokens for names, telephone numbers, addresses, and passwords.
  • Made star ratings announce once as a rating while keeping repeated star glyphs silent.
  • Expanded the public accessibility statement with measures, limitations, and a direct barrier-reporting route.
  • Restricted the website editor to approved photos and videos. Logos, brand marks, decorative icons, service icons, accessibility controls, and interface artwork are excluded.

Continuing content responsibilities

  • Write concise, purpose-based alternative text for meaningful photos and leave decorative images silent.
  • Add accurate synchronized captions to prerecorded video and identify speakers and meaningful sounds.
  • Provide transcripts for instructional media and describe important visual actions not conveyed in speech.
  • Keep links descriptive, form labels visible, errors programmatic, and status information available without relying on color alone.
  • Re-test hosted payment and billing flows after material Stripe changes and maintain a staff-assisted alternative.

Maintenance schedule

  • Every content publish: check alternative text, captions, transcripts, heading order, descriptive links, and mobile presentation.
  • Every code release: run regression tests, build verification, a keyboard smoke test, and runtime review.
  • Monthly: test booking, sign-in, profiles, messaging, learning, and the accessibility contact path.
  • Quarterly: perform a representative WCAG review at narrow widths and 200% zoom, including contrast and focus.
  • Twice yearly: test complete journeys with screen readers and voice-control software.
  • Periodically: involve people with disabilities in usability testing and remediation priorities.
